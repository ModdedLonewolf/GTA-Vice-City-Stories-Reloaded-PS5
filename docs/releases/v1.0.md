The first release of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories Reloaded for PS5: the PS2 game recompiled to run natively on a jailbroken PS5.

New: the whole game, natively on the PS5
- 4K at your TV's resolution, 16:9 widescreen with the HUD at the screen's edges, 2x anti-aliasing and 16x texture filtering.
- 60 FPS, with cars, missions, effects, rain and the radio still at the right speed (the PS2 ran at 30).
- The original logos, intro movie and loading screens.

Controls
- GTA V's layout on the DualSense by default: R2 / L2 to drive or shoot and aim, R1 + L1 for drive-bys, Circle for the cinematic camera, the radio on the d-pad.
- A GTA V weapon wheel: hold L1 and point with the right stick; a tap of L1 puts your weapon away.
- The PS2's own layout is pause menu > Controls > Setup 2. Button prompts and the Controls page follow the layout you use.

Saving
- Autosave to slot 8 after each mission and every 5 minutes in free roam; slots 1-7 are yours. Loading an autosave puts you back where you were, with your weapons.
- The newest save loads at start-up, and the memory card is backed up before every save.

The app
- Its own home screen tile, start screen ("Press X to start") and settings menu (touchpad click + Options).

Two versions, one for each disc
- PPSA94010.zip: for the USA disc (SLUS-21590).
- PPSA94011.zip: for the European disc (SLES-54622). New in this release and much less played than the USA version; languages other than English keep the game's own button words in some prompts.
- Both can be installed side by side; each tells you if you give it the other region's disc.

What you need: a jailbroken PS5 that runs homebrew, and your own Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories for the PS2 (USA, SLUS-21590, or Europe, SLES-54622) as an .iso. No game files are included.

Installing: on RecompStore, install it and pick your .iso on the game's page. By hand: unzip the zip for your disc into /data/homebrew/ and put your .iso in its disc folder (/data/homebrew/PPSA94010/disc/ or /data/homebrew/PPSA94011/disc/).
